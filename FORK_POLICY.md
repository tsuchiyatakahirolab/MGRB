# Fork MGRB Core

Fork it, modify it, build compatible tools, publish academic derivatives, submit
pull requests and reuse it in research or commercial work under Apache-2.0.
Citation is welcome; this policy adds no citation or permission requirement to
the software license. Preserve applicable license notices and upstream data terms.

Generic import, QGIS workflows, evidence-safe processing, public adapters,
transparent public analytics, Build Spec contracts and public verification remain
open research software. We will not intentionally cripple these capabilities to
prevent a fork. Existing published code, themes and examples retain their licenses.

A fork may reproduce the published workflow and appearance. It does not acquire
TSUCHIYA LAB's official hosted-service identity or private signing authority.
Use factual descriptions such as “Based on MGRB” or “Compatible with MGRB”. See
[Trademark Policy](TRADEMARK_POLICY.md) and [Architecture Boundaries](ARCHITECTURE_BOUNDARIES.md).

Hosted-service terms do not remove rights governing public MGRB Core.
