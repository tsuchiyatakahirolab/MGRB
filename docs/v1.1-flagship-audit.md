# v1.1 flagship public-data audit

Audit date: 2026-08-30.

The v1.0 South China Sea flagship remains the v1.1 owner-review flagship. It provides 1,200
geolocated SCSDI event records in the review extent, has CC0 1.0 terms, and demonstrates event
uncertainty without misrepresenting events as tracks.

The audit found useful alternatives but not a demonstrably better legally redistributable
Chinese research-vessel track in the preferred Taiwan/East China Sea/South China Sea/Western
Pacific area:

- PANGAEA 891818 is a rich, CC BY 3.0 Xue Long underway dataset with a documented cruise track,
  but its published positions are Arctic rather than the preferred East Asia area:
  <https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.891818>.
- PANGAEA 939230 is a rich South China Sea underway series, but the vessel is the German R/V
  Sonne rather than a Chinese research vessel: <https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.939230>.
- CCHDO 21OR20080102 is a public East China Sea cruise by Taiwan's Ocean Researcher I, but the
  available integrated products are hydrographic bottle/summary data rather than a richer
  continuous public position track suitable to replace the current flagship:
  <https://cchdo.ucsd.edu/cruise/21OR20080102>.

No private GFW track, licensed AIS, or collector-derived GeoPackage was considered eligible for
the public demo. Replacement was therefore rejected on evidence and license grounds rather than
forced for presentation. PANGAEA Xue Long remains the secondary public track-ingestion and
reproducibility demonstration.
